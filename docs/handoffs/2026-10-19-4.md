## Current state

- Phone test: one track, 11 minutes, 795 m, 145 points, one segment, state
  `completed`. The phone was unplugged, the screen was off, and the walk was laps
  around a house, so all points sit inside about 25 m while the path adds up to
  795 m. Median gap between points 4 s, longest 10 s, no gap over 15 s. Median
  accuracy 4.1 m, worst 24 m. The foreground service survived screen-off on
  battery.
- Not covered by that test: anything over an hour (battery drain, thermal),
  stationary Doze with the phone idle in a pocket, dense tree cover. Battery
  optimisation settings on the Pixel were not checked.
- Emulator: the default GPU mode crashed the emulator once on the map screen. The
  log was full of `gles_v2_imp.cpp ... error null ctx` after MapLibre created a
  Vulkan device, then a SIGSEGV in `libgfxstream_backend.so`
  (`ColorBufferGl::create`, `TextureResize`). With `-gpu swiftshader_indirect`
  the same walk ran cleanly. An earlier emulator crash (Oct 17, other AVD) used
  `-gpu host`. It may have been tied to zooming, which was not reproduced.
- Re-centre note: `enableLocation` adds its tracking listener again whenever the
  style reloads. The extra listeners are harmless but untidy.
- Regions on the phone: `/sdcard/Android/data/ca.mytrails.app/files/regions/`
  holds `roberts-creek.pmtiles` and `roberts-creek-terrain.pmtiles`. One test
  track is saved on the phone and can be deleted from the Tracks screen.
